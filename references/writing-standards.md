# Writing standards

## Voice
- Plain, direct business English. Lead with the conclusion, then the evidence.
- Active voice, concrete nouns, numbers with units. One idea per paragraph.
- One term per concept for the whole document (define it once in a glossary or first use).
- Match the reader: executives get decisions and risks; engineers get exact steps and values; clients get outcomes and next actions.

## Banned patterns (generic AI text)
- Openers: "In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape", "It is important to note", "This document aims to".
- Closers: "In conclusion", "Overall,", "I hope this helps", restating the summary.
- Filler words: leverage, delve, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, holistic, unlock, game-changer, synergy.
- Emojis, exclamation marks, rhetorical questions, unexplained superlatives ("best", "huge").
- Hedging stacks ("may potentially perhaps"). State confidence once, with a reason.

## Claims and numbers
- Every statistic, price, rate, date or quote has a source (URL or document, publisher, collection date). No source = do not state it as fact.
- Label each figure: **Verified** (primary source, dated), **Estimate** (third-party, method stated), **Assumption** (ours, with rationale).
- Never invent figures, references, quotes or links. Missing data: write "Not available" and name where it can be obtained.
- Give confidence (High / Medium / Low) per conclusion plus what would raise it.
- Currency and units explicit (USD, %, per month). Round consistently; totals must equal their parts.

## Structure
- Executive summary first when the document exceeds ~3 pages: purpose, finding, decision or ask, key numbers, top risks. Max one page.
- Heading levels: H1 section, H2 subsection, H3 rare. Titles are noun phrases or findings, not "Introduction".
- Numbered steps for procedures; bullets for parallel items; tables for comparisons of 3+ items across 2+ attributes.
- Every table and chart has a caption and units; every chart has axis titles, and a legend when more than one series.
- Appendix for raw data, methodology, glossary. Sources section at the end.
