# Outlines for other document types
Use with `scripts/build_doc.py` (compose a spec from these headings) or write natively in Google Docs if verified.

- **Business proposal**: Executive summary | Client need (their words) | Proposed solution | Scope and deliverables | Timeline and milestones | Team | Pricing (table, terms) | Assumptions and exclusions | Risks | Next steps and acceptance | Appendix: references and case evidence.
- **Technical documentation**: Overview | Audience and prerequisites | Architecture (diagram + components table) | Setup | Usage / API reference (table: name, type, default, description) | Configuration | Error handling and troubleshooting | Security | Changelog.
- **Research report**: Summary | Question and hypotheses | Method and data | Results (tables/charts) | Analysis | Limitations | Conclusions with confidence | References.
- **Internal company documentation / policy**: Purpose | Scope | Definitions | Policy statements (numbered) | Roles | Procedure links | Exceptions and approvals | Compliance and review | Revision history.
- **Meeting minutes**: Meeting, date, attendees, absentees | Agenda | Decisions (numbered) | Action items (owner, due) | Discussion notes (brief) | Next meeting.
- **Business analysis report**: Summary | Business problem | Current state | Analysis (data, tables) | Options with cost/benefit | Recommendation | Implementation plan | Risks.
- **Project plan**: Objectives and success measures | Scope in/out | Deliverables | Work breakdown | Schedule and milestones | Resources and budget | RACI | Risks and dependencies | Governance and reporting.
- **PRD**: Problem | Goals and non-goals | Users and use cases | Requirements (ID, priority, acceptance criteria) | UX notes | Metrics | Dependencies | Release plan | Open questions.
- **User manual / guide**: Overview | Getting started | Task-based chapters (goal, steps, expected result) | Reference | FAQ and troubleshooting | Glossary.
- **Client-facing report**: Summary of results vs goals | Key metrics (table + chart) | What changed and why | Recommendations | Next period plan. No internal jargon; every number sourced.
- **Strategy document**: Context and diagnosis | Vision and objectives | Strategic choices (and rejected ones) | Initiatives | Metrics and targets | Resources | Risks | Roadmap.
